Engineering Highlights

Built for Driver Precision

Hardware MediaCodec Engine

Direct low-latency H.264 & HEVC hardware encoding targeting up to 60 FPS. Configurable bitrate from 4 Mbps up to 20 Mbps with custom DPI scaling tailored to Tesla's center display.

Bi-directional Touch Bridge

Full multi-touch, pinch-to-zoom, and scrolling gestures transmitted directly back from the Tesla browser into Android through the privileged Shizuku system service without root.

GPS Shield (Navigation Guard)

Safeguards navigation apps (Waze, Google Maps) from satellite anomalies and signal jumps. Seamlessly transitions navigation apps to fused cellular network and Wi-Fi positioning.

Dual Audio Routing

Direct low-latency Bluetooth routing to the car speakers, or uncompressed 16-bit PCM / bandwidth-efficient Opus audio streamed directly through the browser session with delay sync control.

Smart Drive Automations

Auto-start stream when connecting to your Tesla's Bluetooth or designated in-car Wi-Fi. Automatically toggles phone hotspot and dims the physical phone display to save battery.

Multimodal AI Assistant

Built-in hands-free wake word detection ("Hey Tesla"), responsive edge glow visual effects, and Google Gemini live multimodal voice assistance right from the Tesla screen.

Quick Start

Ready in 3 Simple Steps

01

Install & Pair Shizuku

Install TeslaDroid APK and grant privileged shell permissions via Shizuku using Wireless Debugging in Developer Options. No computer and no root required.

02

Connect Car Wi-Fi

Turn on your phone's Wi-Fi hotspot (WPA2). On your Tesla touchscreen, connect to your phone's hotspot and check "Remain connected in Drive".

03

Open Tesla Browser

Open the vehicle Chromium browser, type the displayed short URL (or bookmark your local IP), and enjoy full Android mirroring directly on your dashboard.
